# Devlog

## 2026-10-27

I started this experiment because I wondered whether an AI's problem-solving ability might be blocked or weakened by the premise that a problem is impossible. During the experiment, I realized that, contrary to my hypothesis, this kind of belief is not an obstacle for AI. Instead, I found that when an AI faces a hard problem, it settles for a plausible answer as soon as it finds one. I plan to keep exploring this experiment until I reach the end.
